# Player Guide

You can start playing in under a minute on Discord or Telegram, with the account you already have. This guide can be posted as is in the Discord and Telegram help channels.

## Play on Discord

1. Join the official Mantle Discord server.
2. Go to the #stock-arena channel, or join any voice channel.
3. Click the Activities button (the rocket icon) and choose mStocks Arena. You can also type /arena in #stock-arena.
4. Approve the authorization screen. mStocks Arena only reads your Discord username and avatar.
5. Tap Check in to start your streak.

Playing in a voice channel lets friends join the same screen and play together.

## Play on Telegram

1. Open the official mStocks Arena bot from the link pinned in the Mantle Telegram group.
2. Tap Start, then Play (or the menu button next to the message box).
3. The game opens inside Telegram. It only receives your Telegram name and user id.
4. Tap Check in to start your streak.

Turn on the daily reminder in Settings if you want a nudge when your quiz is ready.

## Your first day

- Check in and read the Stock of the Day.
- Take the Daily Quiz: three questions, 30 seconds each, one attempt.
- Play up to three runs of Higher or Lower.
- Copy your invite link from your Profile and share it.
- See where you rank on the Daily tab of the leaderboard.

Every stock card has a View on Fluxion button that opens the real xStock on Fluxion. Trading there is optional and separate from the game; xStocks are not available in the United States and some other regions.

In Higher or Lower, the server chooses one stock; pick Up or Down for its next
15 minutes. You can keep three runs active with one pending pick each. A correct
pick earns 5 points; a wrong pick ends the run; ten correct complete it. Equal
prices or a void result let you pick again without losing the run. You may leave
and come back: the server stores and settles your pick. Closed markets show
"Waiting for market open" and the scheduled time in UTC; the 15 minutes begin
only after reopening is confirmed. A weekend pick can therefore take much longer
than 15 minutes. Pool quotes do not decide results.

## Stay safe

- mStocks Arena never asks for a wallet, a seed phrase, a private key or a payment.
- Mods never DM you first about prizes. Anyone who does is a scammer: report and block them.
- The only official links are the ones pinned in the Mantle Discord and Telegram.

## FAQ

**Is it free?** Yes. There is nothing to buy.

**Do I need a wallet?** No. You log in with Discord or Telegram.

**Where do the prices come from?** The xStocks public API supplies primary prices every 15 seconds. Fluxion pools on Mantle provide cross-checks and informational on-chain quotes.

**Can I play on both Discord and Telegram?** Yes. For now each platform has its own progress; linking accounts is planned for later in Season 1.

**When does the day reset?** At 00:00 UTC.

**My streak broke. Can a mod restore it?** Only if mStocks Arena itself was down that day. Mods will announce it if so.

**Are points worth money?** No. Points rank you on the leaderboard. Season rewards, if any, are announced by the Mantle team.

## Something is not working

| Problem                               | Try this                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Activity will not open on Discord | Update the Discord app, then try from a voice channel                                     |
| Telegram shows a blank screen         | Close the game, update Telegram and open it again from the bot                            |
| Prices say Paused                     | Price updates are being checked. Games that use prices wait; check-in and quiz still work |
| My points look wrong                  | Open Profile, then History. If something is still off, tag a mod in #stock-arena-support  |
